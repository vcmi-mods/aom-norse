# Age of Mythology: Norse (VCMI town mod)

**神话时代：北欧** — the Norse of *Age of Mythology: Retold* turned into a town for [VCMI](https://vcmi.eu).

## Contents

- 8 dwellings with 24 creature forms: levels 1-5 and 7 each have a base form and two alternative upgrades that can be converted into each other in town; level 6 has two separate lines, the Kraken and Fafnir
- 7 out-of-town units (Hirdman, Huskarl, Raider, Jarl, Draugr, War Boar, Jormun Elver) with three map dwellings
- 4 hero classes (one per major god: Thor, Odin, Loki, Freyr), 28 heroes, the Rune Smith commander
- The Einherjar Warband system: Shield Wall and Svinfylking formations, Einherjar recalled from Valhalla, the stars of Odin's Wain and Ragnarok
- 2 racial secondary skills: Warband Oath and Soul Offering
- 16 god-power spells, plus the Grail spell Gate of the Nine Worlds
- Town: five Ages, building tree, dwellings, mage guild, animated town screen, hall, map look, siege screen, puzzle map, music and boat; special buildings Jotun Altar, Dwarven Armory and Gjallarhorn; the Yggdrasil Grail
- Four giant creature banks (Mountain, Rock, Frost and Fire Giants): their hearts are offered at the Jotun Altar for kingdom-wide bonuses
- Artifacts: the Asgard Hoard set (6 components and 1 combination artifact)
- The Norse Ballista war machine and the Battering Ram, sold weekly at the Dwarven Armory
- Tavern rumors

Submod (optional, enabled by default):

- **Norse Wiki Page** (北欧百科): adds a glossary page to the in-game wiki explaining the Einherjar Warband, Favor, god powers, Ages, Giant Hearts, the Gate of the Nine Worlds and the Battering Ram.

Languages: English (base) and Chinese. Players using any other game language get English.

Sprites are included at 1x only; VCMI scales them up for higher interface scaling.

## Requirements

VCMI 1.8 or later (the mod uses Lua battle scripts). No other mods are required.
